## 1. Executive Summary

This Business Case presents the strategic and financial justification for investing in a **Real-Time Payment Fraud Prevention System (RTPFPS)**. Payment fraud is one of the fastest-growing financial crime vectors globally, and our current detection capabilities are insufficient to address emerging threats at the speed and scale required.

This document outlines the current problem, evaluates three strategic options, recommends the preferred solution, and demonstrates a compelling return on investment with a projected payback period of under 18 months.

| Total Investment | 3-Year Net Benefit | ROI | Payback Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| £1,199,000 | £4,200,000 | 250% | ~17 Months |

> The recommended investment of **£1,199,000** is expected to deliver **£4.2M in quantifiable benefits** over three years.

## 2. Strategic Context & Business Need

### 2.1 Industry Landscape

Payment fraud losses are a critical issue for financial institutions. Organisations without real-time fraud detection are disproportionately exposed to losses from:

- **Card-Not-Present (CNP) fraud**
- **Account Takeover (ATO)**
- **Authorised Push Payment (APP) fraud**
- **Synthetic identity fraud**

All of these have accelerated with the shift to digital payments. Regulators including the **FCA** and **EBA** have also increased scrutiny of fraud controls, making robust real-time monitoring not just a competitive advantage but a **compliance obligation** under PSD2 and the upcoming UK Fraud Strategy frameworks.

### 2.2 Current State Assessment

An assessment of current fraud detection capabilities reveals the following critical gaps:

| Capability Area | Current State | Gap / Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction Monitoring | Batch processing — reviewed every 4 hours | Fraud undetected for hours, leading to unrecoverable losses |
| Detection Method | Manual rule-sets — static, rarely updated | Cannot adapt to evolving fraud patterns; high false negatives |
| False Positive Rate | ~12% of flagged transactions are legitimate | Customer friction, call-centre costs, reputational risk |
| Data Integration | Siloed systems — no unified customer view | Blind spots across channels; no behavioural analytics |
| Analyst Tools | Spreadsheet-based case management | Slow, error-prone, no audit trail, not scalable |
| Regulatory Reporting | Manual monthly reports | Compliance risk — no real-time audit trail for regulators |

## 4. Options Appraisal

Three strategic options have been evaluated against cost, risk, benefit, and strategic alignment criteria.

---

### Option 1 — Do Nothing *(Base Case)*

> Maintain the current batch-based, manual fraud detection process with no significant investment or change.

| ✅ Advantages | ❌ Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| No upfront capital investment required | Continued exposure to £3.59M+ annual losses |
| No disruption to existing operations | Growing regulatory non-compliance risk |
| Familiar processes for current staff | Reputational damage and customer loss |
| | Fraud volumes will increase — losses will grow |
| | No competitive differentiation |

> 🔴 **NOT RECOMMENDED** — Unacceptable ongoing financial and regulatory risk

---

### Option 2 — Buy: Procure a Third-Party Fraud SaaS Platform

> Procure and implement a commercially available, cloud-hosted fraud detection SaaS platform (e.g., FICO Falcon, Featurespace ARIC, or equivalent).

| ✅ Advantages | ❌ Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| Faster deployment (proven platform) | High ongoing SaaS licensing costs (£350K+/year) |
| Vendor manages ML model maintenance | Limited customisation to our specific fraud patterns |
| Lower internal development resource required | Data sovereignty concerns — customer data leaves org |
| Pre-built compliance reporting features | Vendor lock-in risk over multi-year contracts |
| | Integration complexity with legacy core banking still high |

> 🟡 **VIABLE** but cost-prohibitive long term — evaluated further in financial comparison

---

### Option 3 — Build: Internal Development of RTPFPS *(Recommended)*

> Design, build, and deploy a bespoke RTPFPS using internal and contracted engineering, data science, and BA resources, hosted on the organisation's existing cloud infrastructure (AWS/Azure).

| ✅ Advantages | ❌ Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| Full customisation to our fraud patterns and data | Higher upfront investment vs. SaaS option |
| No ongoing SaaS licensing — lower 3-year TCO | Longer delivery timeline (9 months to go-live) |
| Data remains within our infrastructure (GDPR compliant) | Requires skilled internal delivery team |
| Internal IP and competitive advantage | Maintenance and model refresh is our responsibility |
| Scalable and extensible for future fraud types | |
| Builds internal capability and expertise | |

> 🟢 **PREFERRED OPTION** — Best long-term value, full control, and strategic alignment

---

## 7. Key Risks to Business Case

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Financial Impact & Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Benefits not fully realised in Year 1 due to adoption lag | Medium | High | Reduces Year 1 ROI. *Mitigation: Phased rollout with fraud ops change management programme.* |
| Delivery cost overrun beyond 10% contingency | Medium | Medium | Budget increase needed. *Mitigation: Fixed-price contracts for key workstreams; fortnightly budget reviews.* |
| ML model under-performs in production environment | Medium | High | Reduced fraud catch rate. *Mitigation: Champion-challenger model testing; rules engine fallback layer.* |
| Integration delays with core banking | High | High | Delays go-live, extends cost burn. *Mitigation: Early API design workshop; integration ring-fenced in Phase 1.* |
| Regulatory changes alter compliance requirements | Low | Medium | Additional development cost. *Mitigation: Flexible architecture; monthly compliance checkpoint.* |
